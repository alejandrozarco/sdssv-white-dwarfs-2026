## Seven white dwarfs with a Gaia DR3 GLS frequency

`tables/periodic_white_dwarfs.csv` covers seven white dwarfs selected by their Gaia DR3 GLS frequency. Each frequency was recovered in ATLAS or ZTF; the table gives the Gaia and TESS amplitudes and times of maximum at that frequency. Each figure shows the ground-based periodogram (left) and the ground-based and Gaia light curves folded on the adopted frequency (right).
